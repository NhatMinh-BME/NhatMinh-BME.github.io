# Faculty Collaborators

Add faculty members or lecturers you have worked with. Confirm names, titles, affiliations, and links before publishing, and get permission before sharing personal contact details or private collaboration information.

Copy this block for each person:

```markdown
## [Full name]

**Title:** [Professor / Associate Professor / Lecturer / other]\
**Affiliation:** [Department, institution]\
**Collaboration:** [Project, research area, or context]\
**Period:** [Year or date range]

[One or two sentences describing the professional collaboration.]

- Profile: [Institutional profile URL](https://example.com)
- Related work: [Public project, publication, or group URL](https://example.com)
```

If a collaboration is connected to a project or publication on this site, link to that page and describe your own role clearly.
